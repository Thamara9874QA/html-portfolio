The Best Movies According to Thamara

My top 3 movies of all-time

1. Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring

Release Date: December 19, 2001

Director: Peter Jackson

Synopsis: A young hobbit, Frodo, is entrusted with an ancient ring that holds the power to enslave the world. He embarks on a perilous journey to destroy it, accompanied by a fellowship of heroes.

2. Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

Release Date: December 17, 2003

Director: Peter Jackson

Synopsis: The final installment of the epic trilogy, where the forces of good and evil clash in a battle for Middle-earth. Frodo and Sam continue their quest to destroy the One Ring, while Aragorn leads the armies of men against Sauron's forces.

3. The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies

Release Date: December 17, 2014

Director: Peter Jackson

Synopsis: The conclusion of the Hobbit trilogy, where the dwarves, elves, and men unite to face the threat of Sauron's army. Bilbo Baggins must confront his own fears and make difficult choices in the midst of war.
